## 5. Macro & Geopolitical Risk Factors

### 5.1 U.S.-China Trade Relations
The Trump-Xi summit (May 14-15, 2026) is the single most important near-term catalyst. Apple generates roughly **18-20% of its revenue from Greater China** and manufactures the vast majority of iPhones in China through Foxconn and other partners. A trade war escalation or tariffs would directly impact margins and supply chain stability.

### 5.2 Taiwan/TSMC Concentration
As highlighted by Barron's, TSMC sits at the center of a ~$30 trillion market value web. Apple's M-series and A-series chips are fabbed at TSMC (Taiwan). Any geopolitical disruption in the Taiwan Strait represents an existential supply-chain risk.

### 5.3 AI Competitive Landscape
Google, Microsoft/Sam Altman/OpenAI, and Anthropic are all racing ahead. Apple's slower rollout of Apple Intelligence and its more privacy-centric AI approach is viewed by some as a strategic differentiator but by others as falling behind.
