- $101 billion in free cash flow and a 27.2% net margin are world-class, supporting a premium multiple.

**My recommendation: SELL (or take profits).** I'm aligning with the bear analyst.

**Rationale:**
The bear's strongest argument isn't the technical overbought reading—it's the **valuation standing on the shoulders of a Services narrative that depends on hardware unit growth, which is showing cracks.** The bull is correct that Services is a beautiful cash flow machine, but the market is already pricing that perfection at 36x earnings and a 2.58 PEG. That leaves zero room for error. The bear correctly identifies that the "installed base" is static; it's the *growth rate* that matters for Services. If KeyBanc's data is right and hardware demand softens, the whole thesis wobbles.

The technical picture is confirmatory, not primary: extreme overextension above key moving averages, fading volume, and a pattern of corrections after RSI hits the mid-80s. The bear's point about "consensus being dangerous" is the most damning—everyone already loves Apple. That's when the re-rating risk is highest.

1. **Sell your full position into strength.** Specifically, place a limit order to sell at $300-$305. This week's Trump-Xi summit is a perfect catalyst to get out near the top. Don't get greedy waiting for $310.
